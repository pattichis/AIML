```math
   I(i, j) = \cos \left( \frac{2\pi}{N} (u \cdot i + v \cdot j) \right)
```
where $u, v$ represent digital (integer) frequencies.<br>

 B. A greyscale image of a sinusoid that changes from one row to the next.<br>

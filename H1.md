# Problem 4. Max Pooling and downsampling (40 points)
For this problem, refer to the [max pooling example](https://github.com/pattichis/AIML/blob/main/Session_13_Max_Pooling.ipynb).<br>
Here, we want to use the greyscale definitions of dilation and erosion:
```math
\begin{align}
                   & O [i, j] = \text{Dilation} \,(I, S)\, [i, j] = \max_{ (i', j') \in S+(i, j) } I \, [i', j']\\
                   & O [i, j] = \text{Erosion}  \,(I, S)\, [i, j] = \min_{ (i', j') \in S+(i, j) } I \, [i', j']\\ 
     \text{where:} &  & \\
                   & (i', j') \in S+(i, j) = \left \{ (i', j') | \, \text{where}
                              \quad (i', j') = (i, j) + (d_1, d_2) \quad \text{and} \quad (d_1, d_2) \in S \right \} \\
\end{align} 
```

Also, note that for grayscale morphology, dilation is equivalent to max pooling without downsampling.<br>
